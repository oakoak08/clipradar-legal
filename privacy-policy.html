Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Who operates ClipRadar

ClipRadar Studio is currently operated as a local video-workflow project. For privacy questions or requests, contact nadejdec@yahoo.com.

Information processed

Depending on the features you choose to use, ClipRadar may process video files and associated metadata, transcripts, generated clips, draft captions, configuration and local project records. If you connect a platform account, the integration may process the platform account identifier, channel or profile name, OAuth access and refresh tokens, and permissions you authorize. Only information needed for the enabled integration should be requested.

How information is used

Information is used to run the requested video workflow, identify the correct connected account, maintain authorized sessions and, only if separately enabled, prepare or perform platform actions you approve. A connection alone does not mean automatic publishing is enabled.

Where data goes

ClipRadar primarily runs on your local computer. Some features can contact external services, including platform APIs for account connection and any configured video-analysis or discovery services. Those providers process information according to their own policies. Do not assume that every feature is entirely offline.

Storage and security

Project files and connection credentials may be stored locally on the computer where ClipRadar runs. OAuth credentials are sensitive and should not be shared or committed to public repositories. Access to the computer, backups and any configured external services can affect the security of stored information.

Retention and deletion

Local project files and account credentials may remain until you remove them or the application provides a supported deletion workflow. You can request assistance with identifying or deleting locally stored information by emailing the contact address. Revoking platform access through the platform’s account settings may stop future access but may not automatically delete local copies or backups.

Sharing and external providers

ClipRadar does not require selling personal information to operate. Data may be transmitted to third-party services only when an enabled feature requires it, such as OAuth sign-in, platform API calls, or configured processing services. Any future hosted service, analytics or additional integrations should be documented here before launch.

Your choices and requests

You may choose whether to connect supported accounts and can revoke access using the relevant platform’s account settings. For access, correction or deletion questions, contact nadejdec@yahoo.com. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and the circumstances of processing.

Children and updates

ClipRadar is not designed for use by children. This policy will be updated when material data-handling practices change; the effective date should be updated accordingly.
